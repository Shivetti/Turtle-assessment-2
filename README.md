Hello, this is our game Turtle Trouble!

This is a simple, sidescroller game where you play as a sea turtle avoiding trash in the ocean. This game was created for young
children in order to help them become more aware about pollution and littering. 
You will only need one key to play this game, and that is the space key. This key is used to have the sea turtle "jump"
over the numerous obstacles and trash throughout the game.
As you progress, you will have your score tracked on the top right side of the screen, as well as your high score.

Happy playing!
